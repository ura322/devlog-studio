Devlog Studio

Devlog Studio is my personal tool. I run it on my own computer to manage my own YouTube channels: it uploads videos with their titles, descriptions, subtitles and thumbnails, schedules them, reads the channels' analytics and comments, and posts the replies and comment moderation I approve. It has no other users.

Terms of use

Devlog Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Every action that changes something on YouTube (an upload, publishing, a reply, approving or rejecting a comment) runs only after I confirm it.

Privacy policy

  • Devlog Studio uses YouTube API Services. It works only with channels I own, and only after I sign in with my Google account. How Google handles data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • What it reads: my channels' videos, analytics and reporting data, and viewers' comments. Analytics and reporting data are kept on my computer as the channels' history. Viewers' comment texts and names are removed from its files within 30 days.
  • To write reports and draft replies, I have an AI assistant, Anthropic's Claude, read this data on my request (Anthropic Privacy Policy). Otherwise the data is never shared, sold or used for advertising.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored only on my computer. Deleting them, or revoking access on the Google security settings page, removes Devlog Studio's access; deleting local data changes nothing on YouTube.
  • Devlog Studio uses no cookies or trackers.
